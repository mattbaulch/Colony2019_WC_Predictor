📋 Rules & Scoring

How to Play

  1. Pay £10 entry fee to participate
  2. Predict the score for each group stage match (48 matches)
  3. Select your group winners
  4. Make bonus predictions (tournament winner, golden boot, total goals)
  5. Submit your predictions before June 11, 2026

Points System

Correct Home Team Score 5 points
Correct Away Team Score 5 points
Correct Match Result (Win/Draw/Loss) 10 points
Perfect Prediction Bonus (Exact score) 20 points
MAXIMUM per match 40 points
Bonus Predictions (each correct) 10 points

Example

Actual Result: Mexico 2 - 1 South Africa

Your Prediction: Mexico 2 - 1 South Africa

Points Earned:

  • Correct Home Score (2): 5 points
  • Correct Away Score (1): 5 points
  • Correct Result (Mexico win): 10 points
  • Perfect Prediction Bonus: 20 points
  • Total: 40 points

Prizes

  • 🥇 1st Place: 40% of prize pot
  • 🥈 2nd Place: 25% of prize pot
  • 🥉 3rd Place: 15% of prize pot
  • 🏅 Best Weekly Score: £20 (each match week)

Important Information

  • All predictions must be submitted before tournament kick-off on June 11, 2026
  • You can edit your predictions any time before the tournament starts
  • Leaderboard updates after each match
  • 60% of entry fees go to prize pot, 40% to the club
  • Payment must be received before predictions are considered valid
